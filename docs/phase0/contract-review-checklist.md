# Phase 0 — Contract Review Checklist (GroundAlliance)

Review the tenant's GA subscription agreement + order form before Gate 1 closes. Each item: find the clause, quote it verbatim into the evidence file, score it.

| # | Clause | What to look for | Red flag |
|---|--------|------------------|----------|
| 1 | Auto-renewal | Renewal date, notice window (30/60/90 days), notice method (written? portal?) | Window < 30 days or "deemed renewal" language |
| 2 | Early termination | Termination for convenience? Fee? | No convenience exit, or fee > 3 months of fees |
| 3 | Data return on exit | Obligation to return/export customer data, format, timeline, cost | Silent, or "available via professional services at prevailing rates" |
| 4 | Data deletion on exit | Certified deletion timeline after return | Silent — you want deletion AFTER your 30–90 day read-only window |
| 5 | Price escalation | Annual uplift cap or "we may change pricing on renewal" | Uncapped escalation (PE-owned vendors run 8–15%/yr) |
| 6 | Overage terms | $50/250-booking blocks — how measured, when billed | Retroactive tier resets |
| 7 | Add-on bundling | Passenger app, phone masking, GroundXchange priced separately? | Bundled renewal that re-prices everything |
| 8 | SLA / uptime | Any service credit mechanism | None (common at this price point — note it, don't fight it) |
| 9 | Data processing addendum (EU tenants) | GDPR DPA, subprocessor list, EU/US transfer mechanism | No DPA for a Portugal-based operation — GDPR exposure regardless of migration |
| 10 | IP / non-compete | Any restriction on building or using a competing system | Rare but fatal — escalate to counsel immediately |
| 11 | Affiliate terms | Whether farm-in/out partner relationships are contractual with GA or with the partner | GA inserts itself as counterparty — makes affiliate continuity a negotiation, not an export |

## Timing discipline

- The notice window in row 1 defines your **cutover deadline**: cutover must complete, with rollback buffer, before the notice deadline — not before the renewal date. Work backwards: renewal − notice window − 30–90 day read-only period − 2–4 week parallel run = latest build-complete date.
- Diary the notice date in two places the day the contract is read. Missing it costs a full renewal cycle.

## Output

One-page summary per tenant: renewal date, notice deadline, exit cost, data-return posture (good/adequate/hostile), red flags. Feeds directly into gate1-scorecard.md.
