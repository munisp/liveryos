# Phase 0 — GroundAlliance Vendor Diligence: First-Contact Email Script

Purpose: open the Gate 1 diligence conversation with GroundAlliance in writing. Every answer must come back in writing — verbal assurances are not evidence. Target: answers within 10 business days; silence or refusal on items 1–3 is itself a Gate 1 signal.

Send to: GroundAlliance sales/support contact on file for the tenant's account (and info@groundalliance.com). CC the tenant's decision-maker. One email per tenant account under evaluation.

---

Subject: API access, data export, and contract terms — [Tenant legal name], account [ID]

Hello,

We are [Tenant legal name], a GroundAlliance customer on the [plan] plan. We are reviewing our platform architecture for next year and need written answers to the following before [date — 10 business days out].

1. **API access.** Your marketing materials reference documented APIs. Please provide:
   - the current API documentation (REST or otherwise), including authentication model;
   - a sandbox environment or test credentials;
   - a list of supported operations — specifically: create/read/update reservations, read/write customers, read rate cards, read account receivables, and webhooks for reservation status changes.
2. **Data export.** Please confirm in writing which of the following we can export, in which formats, and at what cadence: customer records (with contact details), passenger profiles, reservation history (full field set, including status history and timestamps), rate cards / pricing rules, corporate account hierarchies with PO numbers, driver and vehicle records, open AR balances, and affiliate/farm-out partner terms. If any of these require a professional-services engagement, state the cost and lead time.
3. **Contract terms.** Please confirm our renewal date, the notice window required to prevent auto-renewal, any early-termination provisions, and your data-deletion and data-return obligations on termination.
4. **Integrations.** We note the GroundSpan/GroundXchange/Deem add-on. Please confirm whether it provides machine-readable, two-way booking exchange we can use for parallel-running a second system during a transition period, and its exact pricing on our tier.

We are evaluating this in good faith as a current customer. A complete written response helps us plan responsibly either way.

Regards,
[Name, title, phone]

---

## Operator notes (internal — do not send)

- **Log everything.** Every response, non-response, and delay goes into the Gate 1 scorecard (gate1-scorecard.md). A vendor that will not document export rights in writing is telling you something.
- **GDPR lever (Portugal tenants).** If export answers are evasive, Portuguese/EU tenants may invoke GDPR Article 20 — the right to receive personal data provided to the controller in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format. Escalation path: formal Art. 20 request → CNPD (Comissão Nacional de Protecção de Dados) complaint if stonewalled. Use as leverage, not as opening posture.
- **Nigeria tenants.** NDPR/NDPA 2023 provides analogous subject-access and portability rights; weaker enforcement, but citing it in writing still raises the cost of refusal.
- **Do not threaten termination in the first email.** You want maximum cooperation while you still need read access.
